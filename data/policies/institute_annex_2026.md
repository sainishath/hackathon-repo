# Institute Procurement Annex 2026: Delegation of Financial Powers

**Document ID:** INST-ANNEX-2026  
**Effective Date:** 2026-01-01  
**Version:** 2026  
**Status:** Current  

---

### [INST-2026-DP1] Delegation of Powers: Direct Purchase without Quotation
Under the authority of Goods Manual 2024 (Para 4.12), the Head of Department (HoD) is empowered to sanction direct purchase of goods valued up to INR 50,000 on each occasion without inviting quotations or bids, subject to budgetary allocation. Mandatory documentation: Purchase Indent (FORM-INDENT) and Sanction Order.

---

### [INST-2026-DP2] Delegation of Powers: Purchase by Local Purchase Committee
Under Goods Manual 2024 (Para 4.13), the Dean (R&D / Academic) is designated as the approving authority for procurement of goods valued above INR 50,000 and up to INR 5,00,000. Procurement must be executed through a duly constituted three-member Local Purchase Committee. A minimum of three formal quotations must be obtained and evaluated via a Comparative Statement of Quotations (FORM-CSQ) and Purchase Committee Certificate (FORM-PCC).

---

### [INST-2026-DP3] Delegation of Powers: High Value Tenders and Proprietary Single Source
Procurement of goods exceeding INR 5,00,000 shall be approved as follows:
1. Limited Tender Enquiry (LTE) from INR 5,00,000 to INR 25,00,000: Approving authority is the Director. Minimum of 3 competitive bids required.
2. Advertised Tender Enquiry (ATE / CPPP / GeM) exceeding INR 25,00,000: Approving authority is the Director / Board of Governors. Open public tender mandatory.
3. Proprietary Article / Sole Source Procurement under GFR Rule 166: Irrespective of value, sole source procurements require justification and approval of the Director.

---

### [INST-2026-DP4] Delegation of Powers: Emergency Urgency Procurement
In unforeseen operational contingencies, natural disasters, or critical lab/facility breakdown, the Head of Department (HoD) is authorized to invoke emergency procurement powers for goods valued up to INR 50,000 on each occasion without prior committee constitution. The indenting officer must table a post-facto ratification report to the Director within 48 hours of order placement.
